\section{One directed verifier for all remaining B cases}\label{simp:scalar-B}

B is the complement of $t\le3/25$, $D\le D_A=(21/20)R_4^2$.
Its desired inequality is
$\mathcal E(H)+\Psi(H)+b\le U_\star+(h-1)\lambda_\star$.
We certify the strictly smaller rational allowances
\[
 U_0=.6905799<U_\star,\qquad\lambda_0=.2319048039<\lambda_\star.
\]
The uniform band in Section~\ref{simp:scalar-band} has no external
positive-head premise, so all four bounded covers use the same source
and may be checked independently.

\subsection{The smaller head-score interface}

On B the NEW inequality is redundant. If an eligible NEW head has
$n=y+.4(t-S)<R_4^2$, its eligibility gives
$t\le(3/40)y<(3/40)R_4^2<3/25$ and
$D\le(21/20)y<D_A$, contradicting B. Thus $n\ge R_4^2$ and its
ordinary $.4$ score $n/R_4\ge\sqrt n$ already supplies the NEW bound.
Equality is harmless on the closed cut. Also $c_0t\le1$, so physical
private guards are redundant. Consequently $w_*$ contains only one,
the singleton, the five affine pieces of the two programs, and the joint
score. A conditional piece is used on a box only when enabled everywhere.

\subsection{Root and objective enclosures}

For a box $t\in[t_-,t_+]$, $D\in[D_-,D_+]$, use credit monotonicity:
\[
 H(\mu;t,D)\le C(t_+,D_+,0;\mu)-\mu t_-+1+\log\mu,
\]
\[
 H(\mu;t,D)\ge C(t_-,D_-,0;\mu)-\mu t_++1+\log\mu.
\]
Bisection of the first expression at target zero gives a lower bound
for the clipped multiplier. Testing the second against $P_c/3$ gives
an upper bound. Start with the known admissible endpoint $\min(1,1/t_-)$;
if a capped-target root does not exist, retain that endpoint.
All trials are above the global floor $k/21>1/100$. Inconclusive directed
comparisons widen the interval. If $D\le1$ and
$t\le(1+\log k-P_c/3)/k$, the controller is exactly $k$.

There are two useful ways to pay $b$. A direct bound subtracts the actual
credit lower bound and adds at most $P_c/3$. Alternatively the actual
controller gives $C(t,D,\widehat q;\mu)\ge\mu t-1-\log\mu+b$, hence
\begin{equation}\label{simp:normalized-bound}
 \mathcal E(H)+\Psi(H)+b
 \le\log(\mu V_+)+1-\mu(\beta a+e^{-1}\gamma)
                         -\sum_j\widetilde P_j.
\end{equation}
The same private target cancels exactly. There is no independent boost
coordinate or additional debit in this normalized bound.

Here are complete reductions for the finite box bounds. Put
$Y=\sum y_j$, $S=\sum S_j$, $s=\min(t,S)$, $r=Y-(S-t)^+$,
$e=t-s$, and $\kappa_1=1-2e^{-1}$. Maximizing the direct residual term
in $a,\gamma$ reduces to the larger of
\begin{equation}\label{simp:two-residual-branches}
 \max_{0\le a\le e}[\log(a+r)-\mu\kappa_1a+\mu\beta t],\qquad
 \max_{a\ge e}[\log(a+r)-\mu\beta a+\mu(t-e^{-1}s)].
\end{equation}
Shift mass from $a$ to $\gamma$ at fixed $a+\gamma$ while
$\gamma\le\min(S,t)$; this preserves $V_+$ and lowers the radial cost.
Once $\gamma\ge S$, choose its smallest value allowed by $a+\gamma\ge t$.
Thus $\gamma=\max\{\min(S,t),t-a\}$ gives the two branches.
Each maximum occurs at the clamped value $a=1/(\mu c)-r$ for its
coefficient $c$. The result increases with $t,Y$ and decreases with $S$,
so the appropriate box endpoints give the direct bound. Subtract a
corner credit and headwise price lower bounds, then add $P_c/3$.
The resulting supremum is convex in $\mu$ (a supremum of affine functions
minus concave credit), so multiplier endpoints suffice.

Sharper bounds preserve a price chord or a plane for each head.
For fixed $D$, the five original owner branches are
\[
 \tfrac25\log w,\quad
 \log\frac{w}{1+(5/16)w},\quad
 \log\frac{wy}{y+(5/16)Dw},\quad
 \log\frac{w(1+9w/16)}{1+9w/16+(9w/16)^2},
\]
\[
 \log\frac{w[1+(9/16)Dw/y]}{1+(9/16)Dw/y+[(9/16)Dw/y]^2}.
\]
Each is increasing and jointly concave in its positive arguments.
For the last four this follows by taking logarithms of the concave
functions $x/(1+x)$ and $x(1+x)/(1+x+x^2)$ or their perspectives;
the latter has second derivative $-6x(x+1)/(1+x+x^2)^3\le0$.
The additional localized branch is $(499/1000)g(\log w)$, with the
concave extension from \eqref{simp:price-envelope}. Subtract $K/2$ from
the five original branches and take the maximum with zero.

Compose one branch with one valid concave physical score, never with
a maximum whose concavity is unproved. For a one-dimensional chord
freeze $t=t_-$, $D=D_+$, $S_j=S_{j,+}$. For a two-dimensional plane
use the rectangle in $(y_j,S_j)$ and, in the joint score, the affine
floor $d_sy_j$ in place of its enhanced floor. The joint concavity
then follows from convexity of $E$. A line below both endpoint values,
or a plane below all four vertex values, lies below the chosen concave
price function throughout the interval or rectangle. Retain its maximum
with zero. The owner supremum of a linear function minus this lower
price is attained at polygon vertices or intersections of its edges
with the zero-price line; all are enclosed explicitly.

Use $\log v\le\tau v-1-\log\tau$ in
\eqref{simp:two-residual-branches}, or apply it to $\log(\mu V_+)$ in
\eqref{simp:normalized-bound}. The former retains the credit and adds
$P_c/3$. For the latter the expression is affine in $\mu$ at fixed
comparator coordinates, so its supremum is convex and multiplier
endpoints suffice. In the planar version, $0<\tau\le\beta$ and
$0\le\nu\le\tau$ give the explicit upper bound
\[
 -\log\tau+\mu t\max\{\nu-e^{-1},\tau-\beta\}
 +\sum_j\sup_{y_j,S_j}
        [\mu\tau y_j-\mu\nu S_j-\max(0,\text{price plane}_j)] .
\]
Indeed $-(\sum S_j-\gamma)^+\le-(\nu/\tau)(\sum S_j-\gamma)$,
and $a+\gamma\ge t$, $0\le\gamma\le t$ bound the residual linear
part as displayed. Any rational tangent is valid; floating-point guesses
choose witnesses and subdivision order only.

\subsection{Complete covers, with directed arithmetic}

The verifier covers two closed B rectangles in
$[0,2.500000626]\times[0,21]$, with singleton cutoffs
$Y_h=128,32,6,2$ for $h=1,2,3,4$. Order the $y_j$ without loss of
generality and propagate $0\le S_j\le\min(t,y_j)$.
Private exclusions use \eqref{fd:private-lower}; the delayed profile is
piecewise affine in $D$ at fixed $t$, so its minimum occurs at an endpoint
or a grid crossing. Its rational tail and $c_0t$ give additional exclusions.
A leaf is accepted only by a proved analytic whole-box band, or by a
strict upper bound from the preceding formulas. Otherwise the box is
bisected exactly until all descendants are handled.

All arithmetic uses outward intervals with denominator $I=2^{48}$,
checked 128-bit products and signed directed integer quotients.
Fresh 192-bit Arb enclosures generate the rational input and 36,885
logarithm/loss-table nodes. Logarithms between nodes use lower chords
and upper tangents after dyadic range reduction. The convex decreasing
loss $\varepsilon$ uses upper chords, with its elementary bound one near
zero and $1/(8r)$ in the tail. Localized exponentials use alternating
polynomials with directed remainders. Branch uncertainty takes interval
hulls. Neither tables nor guessed optimizers supply an unverified maximum.
Independent Arb probes additionally audit arithmetic, price, credit and
root enclosures, including absent-capped-root and boundary cases.

\subsection{All unbounded heads}

The owner bound gives $e^{-P_j}\le\min(1,M(D)/y_j)$ and
$P_j\ge(2/5)\log\max(1,y_j)$. From $H(\mu)\ge b\ge0$ and
$C\le\log M(D)$, $\mu\ge e^{-1}/M(D)>e^{-1}/8$.
For a single $y\ge128$, $V_+=a+y-(S-\gamma)^+>125$ and
$125>8/[e^{-1}(1-2e^{-1})]$. The residual expression decreases with $a$,
so set $a=(t-\gamma)^+$. Its derivative in $\gamma\in[0,t]$ is
$\mu(1-2e^{-1})$ or $\mu(1-2e^{-1})-1/V_+$, both positive.
Thus $(a,\gamma)=(0,t)$ is optimal and
\begin{equation}\label{simp:tail-gate}
 \mathcal E_1+\Psi+b
 \le\mu\beta t+\log M(D)-C(t,D,0;\mu)+K/2+b.
\end{equation}
A separate two-dimensional directed cover proves this below $U_0$ on
the entire private-feasible domain. On a box, bisect the sufficient
upper-root test $C(t_-,D_-,0;v)-vt_++1+\log v\ge P_c/3$.
Its resulting upper enclosure $v_+$ gives the rigorous bound
\[
 v_+\beta t_++\log M(D_+)-C(t_-,D_-,0;v_+)+K/2+P_c/3,
\]
since credit slopes in $v$ are at most $e^{-1}t_-<\beta t_+$.
Private-infeasible boxes are discarded, and unresolved boxes bisected.
The complete replay visits 411 boxes, with 130 accepted and 76 excluded
leaves, depth at most 14. Its upper bound is
$.690423443261483775220432280686<U_0$, a margin greater than $.00015645$.

For $h\ge2$, submodularity gives
$\sum_{j\in H}V_+(H\setminus\{j\})\ge(h-1)V_+(H)$.
Keeping the actual target, HOME credit and prices fixed, exponentiation
of the smaller-count inequalities gives
\[
 \mathcal E_h+\Psi+b\le U_0+(h-2)\lambda_0+
               \log\frac{\sum_j e^{-\widetilde P_j}}{h-1}.
\]
Every inverse charged price is at most one. A head $y\ge Y$ permits
this deletion whenever
$e^{K/2}Y^{-2/5}<(h-1)(e^{\lambda_0}-1)$.
The exact directed gaps for $(h,Y)=(2,32),(3,6),(4,2)$ exceed
$.01095,.03354,.02499$, respectively. Induct from the complete one-head
bound, then two, three and four. This closes all B states with one
through four heads; zero heads were proved by the controller theorem.
