\section{One controller and a final rectangular classification}
\label{simp:algorithm-interface}

The graph theorem needs a class of compatible rows, but the online
update does not.  This section gives the exact and finite interfaces
that separate these two roles.  It uses the score facts in
Section~\ref{simp:score-primitives}, the compatible minorants in
Section~\ref{simp:minorants}, and the scalar controller in
Section~\ref{simp:controller-section}.  All constants displayed as finite
decimals are exact rational numbers.

Write $z=e^{-1}$, $\beta=1-z$, $k=1/(4\beta)$,
$R_4=1.00032357$, $L=\log R_4$, and
\[
 T_A=\frac3{25},\qquad
 D_A=\frac{21}{20}R_4^2,\qquad
 D_m=\frac{26267}{25000},\qquad
 P=\frac{43}{25}L.
\]
At an iterate $x$, solve the cost-two LP of
\eqref{simp:hybrid-lp} and retain one complementary optimal pair.
Set $u_i=e^{v_i}$, $t_i=F_i/u_i$, $D_i=\alpha_i/u_i$, and
$\sigma_i=\log(u_i/T_{i0})$.  The private score $T_{i0}$ and all
physical scores use this same residual point and this same dual.

\subsection{The uniform online controller}

Write $C(t,D;\mu)=C(t,D,0;\mu)$ for the HOME credit from
Section~\ref{simp:controller-section}, and put
\[
 H(\mu;t,D)=C(t,D;\mu)-\mu t+1+\log\mu,
 \qquad \underline\mu(D)=\frac{k}{\max(1,D)}.
\]
The credit is a minimum of expressions of the form $\mu q+c_q$,
with $q\ge0$.  Hence $H$ is increasing on
$(0,\min(1,1/t)]$.  On private-feasible states the scalar theorem
guarantees that its endpoint reaches each target used below.
Use the single rule
\begin{equation}\label{simp:uniform-controller}
 b_i=\frac13\min(\sigma_i,P),\qquad
 \mu_i=\max\{\underline\mu(D_i),r_{b_i}(t_i,D_i)\},
 \qquad H(r_b;t,D)=b.
\end{equation}
The subsequent radial selector and residual update do not
differentiate this root, the auxiliary LP, or a chosen optimum.

\begin{lemma}[The boosted root already equals the floor on A]
\label{simp:uniform-floor}
For $0\le t\le T_A$ and $0\le D\le D_m$, every target
$0\le b\le P/3$ gives $\mu=\underline\mu(D)$ in
\eqref{simp:uniform-controller}.
The assertion remains valid for $t\le T_A+10^{-10}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The localized transfer has branches
\[
 \log M(e^{\pi-\rho}),\quad \frac35\pi,\quad h(\pi),
 \qquad
 M(s)=1+\min\left\{\frac{(9s/16)^2}{1+9s/16},\frac5{16}s\right\},
\]
where, with $a=1/1000$,
\[
 h(s)=s-\min\{(1-e^{-10s})/10,(1-e^{-10a})/10\}.
\]
The maximum decrease of the last branch below $(3/5)s$ is
$(1-e^{-10a})/10-(2/5)a<3/5000$.  Indeed the difference
increases up to $a$ and decreases after $a$.
The HOME profile is at least $\max(1,D)$, and
$M(1)=481/400>D_m$.  Thus, for $1\le D\le D_m$,
$C(t,D;\mu)\ge(3/5)\log D-3/5000$; for $D\le1$ the
credit is nonnegative.  At the floor, in both cases,
\[
 H(\underline\mu;t,D)\ge
 1+\log k-\frac25\log D_m
       -k(T_A+10^{-10})-\frac3{5000}
 >\frac1{250}>\frac P3.
\]
The floor lies below $\min(1,1/t)$, since $k<1$ and
$k(T_A+10^{-10})<1$.  Monotonicity of $H$ proves the claim.
\end{proof}

Consequently the same controller supplies the A floor and the B
private-gap budget.  No online branch tests row membership.

\subsection{One rectangle, tested only at the selected iterate}

After the online process selects an iterate uniformly, define
\begin{equation}\label{simp:rectangular-A}
       A=\{i:t_i\le T_A,\ D_i\le D_A\},\qquad B=[n]\setminus A.
\end{equation}
All supported NEW labels lie in this rectangle.  Indeed a NEW score
$N=p+(2/5)d$ has the same-head ordinary score $N/R_4$, so its
complementary constraints imply $\pi\le L$ and $N/u=e^{2\pi}\le R_4^2$.
Its eligibility conditions $F\le(3/40)p$ and
$\alpha\le(21/20)p$, together with $p\le N$, now give
\[
       t\le(3/40)R_4^2<T_A,\qquad D\le D_A<D_m.
\]
The private bound $T_{i0}\le F_i+(37/100)\alpha_i$ gives
$T_{i0}/u_i\le T_A+(37/100)D_m<1$ on A, so A has no private mass.
The minorant theorem applies on the slightly larger rectangle
$t\le T_A,D\le D_m$; it covers every supported ordinary label
and every small-deficit NEW repair candidate.  In particular it
permits the one ordinary plus one NEW, or two NEW, choices in the
network.  All B rows have no supported NEW and use one original
program after repair.  Thus \eqref{simp:rectangular-A} satisfies
the full local contract of Theorem~\ref{simp:repair-theorem}.

The online procedure is therefore just: form the scores, solve one
LP, evaluate \eqref{simp:uniform-controller}, and make the radial
update.  At its one selected iterate, classify by
\eqref{simp:rectangular-A}, sample one ordinary matching, solve one
minimum-cost flow, run the common forest rounding, and rematch the
reserved items.  Section~\ref{simp:graph} proves the conditional
expected-log guarantee of this one matching sample; no repaired
component scores are compared.

\subsection{A finite interface with strict, one-sided tests}

Here are all additional local tolerances required by these
simplifications.  Let $0<\eta\le\eta_0=10^{-10}$.  On an accurate
history, let every ordinary and private rational log weight be an
upper bound with error at most $\eta$.  Define NEW weights from the
individual conditional $.4$ entry, before any maximum, by
\[
       n_{ij}=w^{(.4)}_{ij}+\Lambda,
       \qquad L\le\Lambda\le L+\eta.
\]
Thus $\log N_{ij}\le n_{ij}\le\log N_{ij}+2\eta$ and every
supported NEW price satisfies $\pi_j\le\Lambda$ exactly.
Use a lower confidence endpoint in the raw eligibility test;
every true eligible label is then present, and an additionally
enabled label has $F\le(3/40)p+\eta u$.

Obtain enclosing intervals $[t^-,t^+]$, $[D^-,D^+]$ of width
at most $\eta$.  The finite final classifier is
\begin{equation}\label{simp:finite-rectangle}
       t^-\le T_A,\qquad D^-\le D_A+6\eta.
\end{equation}
The constant $D_A$ is evaluated as its exact rational value. Since
$D_Ae^{2\eta}\le D_A+6\eta$ and
$(3/40)R_4^2e^{2\eta}+\eta<T_A$, this includes every supported
rational NEW label.  Every classified A row satisfies
\[
       t\le T_A+\eta,\qquad
       D\le D_A+7\eta<D_m.
\]
Every classified B row belongs to the exact complement of
\eqref{simp:rectangular-A}.  There is no adverse B boundary strip.
Lemma~\ref{simp:uniform-floor} covers the small additional A strip
with the same boosted target.

Use rational repair thresholds
\[
 K-\eta\le K_0\le K,\qquad
 T-5\eta\le T_0\le T-4\eta,\qquad
 P_0=\Lambda+(3/10)T_0,
\]
where $K=7L/6$ and $T=12L/5$.
Then $P_0\le P$, and the strict comparisons
\[
 \Lambda<K_0,\qquad
 \frac P3<\frac{K-\eta_0}{2},\qquad
 \frac P3<\frac6{25}(1-e^{-(T-5\eta_0)})
\]
give exactly the finite graph contract.  Set
$\widehat c_{ij}=(v_i+2\pi_j-n_{ij})^+$.  These computed deficits
do not exceed the true deficits.  Every $\widehat c\le T_0$ head
satisfies the broad minorant condition $\alpha+F^+\le5p/3$:
\[
 \frac p u\ge e^{-T_0-2\eta}-\frac25t
 \ge R_4^{-3}(1-2\eta)-\frac25(T_A+\eta)
 >\frac35(D_m+T_A+2\eta).
\]
The definition of $n$ is used only when $\alpha\le4p$.
Otherwise $N/u<D/4+(2/5)t<R_4^{-3}(1-2\eta)$, so the head
already has a deficit above $T$ and may be omitted.
The minorant proof's strict exclusions also survive these
tolerances: a supported ordinary score is at least $ue^{-\eta}$;
a score within $\eta$ of its singleton may be replaced by that
constant at cost $\eta$, and every remaining supported score
has the specified additive minorant.  All repaired row weights
still sum to one, so score overstatements and constant replacements
cost at most $2\eta$ per row.  The graph ledger itself is dominated
one-sidedly as proved in Section~\ref{simp:graph}.

For the only remaining A boundary, set
$u'=u\max(1,t/T_A)$, leaving all item prices fixed.
Then $t'\le T_A,D'\le D_m$, and
$s=\log(u'/u)\le\eta/T_A=(25/3)\eta$.
This is a comparison point, not another LP solve.  The floor obeys
\[
 \frac\mu u=\frac{k}{\max(u,\alpha)}
 \ge\frac{k}{\max(u',\alpha)}=\frac{\mu'}{u'}.
\]
The logarithmic comparator term changes by $s$.  The dimensional
radial coefficient has negative part at most $\beta F$, so the
scalar expression changes by at most
\[
 s+\beta F\left(\frac{k}{\max(u,\alpha)}
          -\frac{k}{\max(u',\alpha)}\right)
 \le(1+t/4)s<9\eta.
\]
Arithmetic deficits increase at $u'$; price and count charges stay
fixed.  Thus this bound also covers the critical equality face.

Finally, monotonicity of $C$ gives, for
$0<\mu_1<\mu_2\le\min(1,1/t)$,
\[
 H(\mu_2)-H(\mu_1)
 \ge\log(\mu_2/\mu_1)-t(\mu_2-\mu_1)
 \ge\tfrac12(\mu_2-\mu_1)^2.
\]
Credit and target enclosures of radius $16\eta$, together with
bisection to width $\eta$, therefore give an upper multiplier
with error at most $32\sqrt\eta$.  No derivative lower bound at
an endpoint is needed.  Since the normalized radial coefficient
is at least $-\beta t$ and private feasibility gives
$t\le1/.3999999$, the resulting scalar loss is at most
$32\beta\sqrt\eta/.3999999$.  Together with the preceding
$2\eta+9\eta$ terms this is below $64\sqrt\eta$ per row.
The global precision schedule need only pay this explicit loss,
the score primitive's sampling error, and the forest error of
Lemma~\ref{simp:forest}.  It does not depend on a positive support
lower bound, continuity of the selected LP solution, or the smallest
numerical scalar margin.
On an inaccurate history, retain feasibility by checking the absence
of private mass on A and the required network bounds before repair;
if a check fails, use the reserved matching.  The known reservation
and full-set log values bound the loss of every such history.
